The grocer still writes debts on paper.

He forgets who owes what, mixes up two Karims, and has no idea what actually sells. He will not type while three customers wait.

S
SoukVoiceThe Karna that listens to Darija

He speaks. The ledger updates.

  • Voice or text in Darija becomes a structured sale.
  • The customer balance is updated, and a WhatsApp reminder is ready in one tap.
  • A business panel shows what to restock, what to stop buying, and whom to chase.

Demo · 90 seconds

One journey. No guessing.

  • Play a recorded phrase: “Karim khda tlata kilo dial sokkar…”
  • Two customers are named Karim. The tool asks which one.
  • Confirm → new balance, WhatsApp message, Today list, and “Ton business”.

How the AI is actually used

Darija in. Honest JSON out.

Gemini first

Turns spoken or typed Darija into a sale: client, items, paid, credit, confidence.

Groq if Gemini is down

Whisper for speech, then an open model for extraction. Same JSON contract.

It can refuse

Duplicate first names stay separate. Low confidence is labelled “à confirmer”.

No Brev bonus claimed

Judging is tool-neutral. We disclose the stack, the fallback, and what is live.

Tests and limits

What we can stand behind.

  • Recorded Darija phrases and an invented 3-week ledger. No real customers.
  • Numbers in “Ton business” are calculated from the Karna, not guessed.
  • WhatsApp is a prefilled tap, not automatic sending.
  • Live microphone can fail in a noisy room. The video uses prepared audio.

Next

Automatic reminders. Orders by voice.

Then several shops, so a grocer can see what moves in his own street — still without typing.
